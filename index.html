How to play

Beat the five rivals one by one, first to the finish line. Win to earn cash, buy upgrades in the Garage, then outrun the police.

Desktop · keyboard

← → or A DSteer
↑ or WGas

Mobile · touch pads

Left / RightHold to steer
GasHold to accelerate. Let go to coast
BrakeHold to slow down
NitroHold for a speed boost
Auto-gasTick the box under the pads to keep the gas on automatically

Tip: turn your phone sideways for a bigger view.

Tips

Stay on the road, since grass slows you down. Hitting traffic costs speed. Save nitro for straights. In the police chase, keep moving. If the police stay close behind you for 2 seconds, you are busted.
